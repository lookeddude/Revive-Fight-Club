# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Fitness enthusiasts, beginners wanting to learn combat sports, and professional fighters in Bengaluru. They are looking for high-quality training, expert coaching, and a premium gym environment.

## Product Purpose
A promotional and informational website for Revive Fight Club. It serves to showcase the gym's combat sports programs (MMA, BJJ, Boxing, Muay Thai, etc.), introduce the expert trainers, display membership pricing, and drive conversions by allowing visitors to book a trial class.

## Positioning
Bengaluru's premier combat sports gym with professional fighters as coaches, offering authentic combat sports training in a premium facility, distinct from typical commercial fitness gyms.

## Operating Context
Visitors typically browse the site to evaluate the gym before visiting in person, comparing pricing, viewing the schedule, and reading about the trainers. The primary action is booking a paid trial class via WhatsApp or contact forms.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Trial classes are strictly charged at Rs 1000 per session (no free trials).
- Fully responsive web platform.
- Content must be easily editable by admins via a built-in admin panel.
- Opening hours: Monday – Saturday.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Revive Fight Club (RFC)
- Visual Theme: Premium dark/black theme (`#0a0b0a`, `#111210`) with a bold orange-red accent (`#ff571a`).
- Logo: Transparent dark-theme logo (`public/images/rfc-logo-dark.png`) and RFC favicon.
- Typography: Outfit (headings, bold, uppercase) and Inter (body text, structured data).
- Voice: Professional, bold, encouraging, and premium.
